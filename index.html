What this is

Offshore, a seismic vessel costs around $250,000 a day and a rig waiting to get on location around $500,000. If two operations meet in the same patch of sea, one of them waits.

Every survey, rig move, well intervention and pipeline job on the UK shelf needs a consent from the regulator (OPRED), and the list is public. SIMOPS Radar reads that list, finds activities in the same or a neighbouring block whose consent windows overlap, and ranks them by how likely they are to matter.

Pick an operator to see only the clashes that involve its own work. Turn the sensitivity down to see the few that are worth a phone call.

Limits: locations are licence blocks, not exact positions. Dates are consent windows, which are usually wider than the actual work. Survey notices have no end date, so 30 days is assumed.
