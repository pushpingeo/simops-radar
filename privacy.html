Privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026 (product updates added)

SIMOPS Radar is a small independent project. You can use all of it without giving us anything. The page sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics.

What we keep, and only if you give it to us

  • Alert sign-up. Your email address, the company you picked, what you were watching, the settings link you had at the time, and the date. We use it to tell you when email alerts start, to send the alerts you asked for, and now and then to share a product update about SIMOPS Radar. That means a handful of emails a year at most, never marketing for anyone else.
  • Feedback you send. Your thumbs up and down marks, your notes and, only if you add it, your email so we can reply.
  • Abuse protection. A scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, kept for one day to limit how often the forms can be used. It can't be turned back into your IP address.

Marks you make on clashes stay in your own browser until you choose to send them.

Why we're allowed to

Because you agreed, by ticking the box or choosing to send. You can withdraw that at any time.

Where it lives and who sees it

It's stored with Cloudflare, which runs the small server behind the forms. Only the SIMOPS Radar team can see it. We don't sell it or share it with anyone, and we won't add you to any other mailing list.

How long

Until you ask us to remove it, or until SIMOPS Radar stops running, whichever comes first. We review the list at least once a year and delete anything that's no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see what we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted. Use the Remove me button in the sign-up box, or email hello@simopsradar.com. Every email we send will have a one-click way out.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Rob Dunfey, who runs SIMOPS Radar. Contact: hello@simopsradar.com.
