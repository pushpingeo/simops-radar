Privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026

SIMOPS Radar is a side project run by Rob Dunfey. You can use all of it without giving me anything. The page sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics.

What I keep, and only if you give it to me

  • Alert sign-up. Your email address, the operator you picked, what you were watching, the settings link you had at the time, and the date. I use it to tell you when email alerts start, and later to send the alerts you asked for. Nothing else.
  • Feedback you send. Your Real / Not real marks, your notes and, only if you add it, your email so I can reply.
  • Abuse protection. A scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, kept for one day to limit how often the forms can be used. It can't be turned back into your IP address.

Marks you make on alerts stay in your own browser until you choose to send them.

Why I'm allowed to

Because you agreed, by ticking the box or choosing to send. You can withdraw that at any time.

Where it lives and who sees it

It's stored with Cloudflare, which runs the small server behind the forms. Only I can see it. I don't sell it or share it with anyone, and I won't add you to any other mailing list.

How long

Until you ask me to remove it, or until I stop running SIMOPS Radar, whichever comes first. I'll review the list at least once a year and delete anything that's no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see what I hold about you, correct it or have it deleted. Use the Remove me button in the sign-up box, or email hello@simopsradar.com. Every alert email will have a one-click way out.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
